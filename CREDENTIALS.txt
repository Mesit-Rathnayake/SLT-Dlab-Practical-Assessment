2. AUTHORIZATION & NAVIGATION BAR ENFORCEMENT
================================================================================

The system enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
  • Unauthenticated: Portal tabs are hidden; visitors must sign in.
  • SUPER_ADMIN: Navigation shows "Super Admin" and "General Admin" tabs.
  • ADMIN: Navigation shows "General Admin" tab only. Doctor Portal and Super Admin
    tabs are hidden and guarded.
  • DOCTOR: Navigation shows "Doctor Portal" tab only. Administrative portals
    are hidden and guarded.

Reviewer Convenience:
  On the Sign In page and modal, a "Reviewer Quick-Fill" helper is provided to
  populate the email and password fields with a single click. When submitted,
  the credentials are fully verified against the Go backend and PostgreSQL
  database with bcrypt password hashing and JWT token issuance.

3. PATIENT RECORDS & NIC (NATIONAL IDENTITY CARD) SYSTEM
================================================================================

In accordance with hospital administrative security, patient records are managed
internally by clinical staff:
  - Unique Primary Identifier: National Identity Card (NIC) number.
  - Appointment Booking Workflow:
    1. Click "+ Schedule Appointment".
    2. Enter Patient NIC (e.g. 198810502341).
    3. System verifies NIC against database.
    4. If existing patient: displays previous consultation history and autofills demographics.
    5. If new patient: registers permanent patient record linked to that NIC.
    6. The booked appointment is automatically linked to the patient's chronological history.
